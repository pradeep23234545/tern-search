Tern

Private search through your own server.

Examples: 192.168.1.20:8888 · 100.64.0.5:8888 (Tailscale) · https://search.example.org

Where do I find the address?

  1. On your computer, open the Tern desktop app.
  2. Go to Settings → Use Tern on your phone and turn on Share with devices on my Wi-Fi.
  3. Type the address it shows here. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Away from home? Install Tailscale (free) on both devices and use your computer's Tailscale address.
